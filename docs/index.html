02

Where the boundary sits

Your rules, enforced by the operating system.

On macOS, within the proven scope, enforcement does not depend on the agent’s cooperation.

The macOS operating-system wall requires a separate signed app, system extension and privileged setup; adding Sanctuary to an MCP configuration does not complete that setup.
