Starting today I'm trying something new on this blog. Everything I publish here from October 11, 2026 onward will come in four layers, each clear about who (or what) wrote it.

**Layer 1: The claim.** The first thing you read is about the length of an X post. It is the whole piece in one breath. I write it.

**Layer 2: The summary.** Next come a few paragraphs: what the piece argues, why now, and what I am asking you to believe or do. I write that as well.

**Layer 3: The essay.** The long piece, written for a person to read. Claude (or another model) drafts it under my direction, from my argument, my notes, and research we did together. I edit it before it goes out. If it's wrong, that's on me; I approved it.

**Layer 4: The machine layer.** Under the essay sits a long structured section that most people will never read. It lists every source, maps each factual claim to its evidence, records the reasoning behind the piece, including the alternatives I rejected and why, lists what I could not verify, and keeps the version history. Claude produces it. It exists so that your AI agent, if you send one, can check the piece rather than take my word for it.

Why do it this way? Almost everyone now writes with AI, and almost everyone pretends they don't. I would rather be transparent when I do. The parts that carry my voice and my judgment are the parts I write by hand. The parts that carry the fact-checking are published in full so you can run them yourself.

A single line under every title repeats this structure, so you never have to guess. Claude is a tool I use the way I use a research assistant or a word processor. I am the author of everything on this site, and I answer for all of it.

This standard began on October 11, 2026, though I may go back and retroactively edit previous essays. When I do that, I'll be transparent about that as well.
